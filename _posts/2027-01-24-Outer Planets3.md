<div align="center"><mark><span style="color: blue;">In astronomy, the term outer planets refers to the planets that are farther from the Sun than the asteroid belt. While Jupiter and Saturn have been known since ancient times, the discovery of Uranus and Neptune required modern technology and mathematics. Mars is not an outer planet, even though it is farther from the Sun than Earth.</span></mark></div>

><span style="color: white">**The message on the Pioneers**</span>

The Pioneer 10 and Pioneer 11 each have an identical plaque on board with a message in visual language. It is an example of simplex information to which there is probably no answer. 

That plaque is made of anodized aluminum. This created an artificial, durable and protective oxide layer on the outside.

It is 152 mm by 229 mm in size and the thickness is about 1.2 mm. She weighs 120 grams. 

It is attached to the outside of the probe on one of the support beams on which the satellite dish is attached. This position provides protection against erosion by interstellar dust.

This plaque, designed by Carl Sagan, serves as a kind of "interstellar bottlepost" and is intended for an eventual extraterrestrial intelligence that could find the probes millions of years in the future. 

The message is only in the form of engraved diagrams and symbols.

We first give the full picture and then go into each part separately.

><span style="color: white">**In detail**</span>

<div align="center"><img src="/images/P Detail 1.jpg" alt="" width="" height=""></div>

<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 12pt;"><span style="color: black;"><i>This illustration can be seen on the Wikipedia website. Notes have been added.</i></span></p>

This is the representation in which a hydrogen atom changes from one, slightly lower energy state (left) to another, slightly higher energy state (right), due to the absorption of a photon.

The reverse is also possible. So switching from a higher energy state (right) to a lower energy state (left) where a photon is then emitted.
Both the proton and the electron have a property called 'spin'. They can have two possible spin orientations relative to each other:
* Parallel: the spins point in the same direction. This is the state with a slightly higher energy (right).
* Antiparallel: the spins point in the opposite direction. This is the state with a slightly lower energy (left).

When a hydrogen atom in the parallel state spontaneously 'flips' to the more stable, anti-parallel state (a so-called spin-flip), it emits a photon with a very specific amount of energy. 

That energy has a precise wavelength of 21.1 cm per 0.7 nanosecond. That is a radio signal and it can be picked up.

This radiation is crucial for radio astronomy, it allows us to detect hydrogen.

The extremely stable and precise nature of this transition is also used as the basis for highly accurate atomic clocks.

This fundamental and universal physical constant is thus used as a universal unit of measurement in distance and time.

The hydrogen molecule H2 with 1 proton in the nucleus and 1 electron around the nucleus is without a doubt the most abundant element in space. It is at the basis of the creation of all other elements after the necessary nuclear fusions. It does indeed emit radio waves with a length of 21.1 cm, but will one be coupled to the other?

A man and a woman are schematically depicted, they have tried to merge some breed characteristics. The original lines of the figures are based on drawings by Leonardo da Vinci and Greek sculptures.

The man's right hand is raised as a sign of good will, say a greeting. While this gesture may not be/misunderstood, it provides a way to show the opposable thumb and how the limbs can be moved. Critics see the initiative of the man as the subordinate role of the woman. The same critics believe that the greeting gesture that is universally clear to us can just as well be interpreted as a declaration of war or an insult.

2 genera are presented. But what to do with a parthenogenetic society?

Originally it was meant that the humans would be shown hand in hand, but it was soon realized that an alien could see them as a single being instead of two organisms.

The height of the woman is indicated between the horizontal lines with the binary equivalent of eight. We read from bottom to top (which is in fact from left to right when we tilt the photo) and see that there is an etching error in the first horizontal line. So we see 1 vertical bar and 3 vertical bars. Instead of the well-known "1" and "0", "l" and "–" are used.

We then read 1000, which corresponds to (1 X 23) + (0 X 22) + (0 X 21) + (0 X 20) and thus yields 8. If we take the unit of 21.1 cm times 8, we get 168.8 cm, which corresponds to the average height of a woman.

The icon on the left of the plate shows fifteen lines that come from the same origin, being the position of the sun. 

Fourteen lines are made up of dashes representing a binary number. These numbers correspond to the frequency with which the pulsars emit their energy. So far, 14 such pulsars have been discovered from our observation site. This is how we indicate our position. Even though we have to take into account that more pulsars will be discovered.

Because the frequency of the pulsars also changes as time passes, the time of launch/transmission can also be determined.

The fifteenth line, without a binary number, extends horizontally beyond the human images and then shows the distance to the center of the Milky Way once.

This performance also appears on the Voyager Golden Record (see next article).

At the bottom of the plate our solar system is schematically depicted. 

Saturn has drawn a line representing the rings, as an additional clue to identify the solar system.

The image is not to scale, the dimensions of the planets differ more in reality and so do the distances between them. Our solar system is correctly displayed in order, but the proportions are not correct. The small planets should actually be invisible.

Note that at that time Pluto was still considered a planet. 

An arrow shows the trajectory of the Pioneer probe from the third planet (Earth), which passes Jupiter and leaves the Solar System. So we see the probe and her orbit. 

There is a problem with the image size of the probe/capsule. It is shown here in miniature. What is her real size? One might think that our Earth is ridiculously small or that the probe is absurdly large.

The binary numbers always give the relative distance of the planets from the Sun. The unit here is suddenly a tenth of the distance between Mercury and the Sun, being 5.8 million kilometers. Suddenly, it is no longer allowed to count with 21.1 cm. Even we have to think about this, it is gradually becoming a heavy puzzle. We check for a moment. The binary number 11010 is next to the earth, which corresponds to 26. So 5.8 million kilometers times 26 does indeed correspond to about 150 million kilometers which is our distance from the sun. This check is also correct for the other distances in binary values.

Note: We would like to mention that the plaque is 15.2 cm by 22.9 cm in size. Surely one could have taken 21.1 cm by 21.1 cm?
